**Why there's no background video:** no downloadable Pexels clip could be verified as
Washington, DC. Generic river clips were rejected because they show other cities. The hero uses a
gentle slow-zoom on a Potomac River photo instead; it's turned off for visitors who prefer
reduced motion. To add a real DC video later, set `media.heroVideo.mp4` and `media.heroVideo.webm`
in `site.config.mjs`.
